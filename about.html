Frequently Asked Questions

How do Solana payments work?

We use Solana blockchain for payments, which offers quick transactions and low fees. Connect your Phantom wallet, select a service, and make a secure payment. The payment is held in escrow until your boost is completed successfully.

Is my account information safe?

Absolutely. We implement strict security protocols and our boosters sign NDAs. We never store your passwords, and we use secure methods to access your account only for the boosting period.

How are your boosters selected?

We have a rigorous selection process where less than 5% of applicants are accepted. All boosters must be in the top 1% of players in their game, pass a skills test, and have their identity verified.

What if I'm not satisfied with the service?

We offer a satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with the service, contact our support team within 48 hours of completion, and we'll either fix the issue or provide a refund according to our refund policy.
